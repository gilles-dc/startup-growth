# ICP deliverable — format and examples

The format the founder produces. Specific enough that a stranger could find this person.

## B2B example — EU fintech compliance automation tool

| | |
|---|---|
| **WHO** | Series A–B EU fintech (payments, neobank, crypto), 20–80 employees, €1M–10M ARR. Just hired its first CCO. Backed by Seedcamp, Earlybird or an equivalent EU VC. No compliance tooling: runs on Sheets and Notion. |
| **WHAT** | Google Sheets for tracking, email for evidence. A new-category purchase: replacing manual work, not a competitor. Discovers solutions through a VC portfolio intro or a peer recommendation at Money20/20. |
| **WHY** | Regulatory audit or licence application in a new EU market. Fines of €50K–€2M per violation. Existential risk, not efficiency. Trigger: a regulatory notice, a Series A compliance clause, or the first compliance hire. |
| **HOW** | Buyer = COO or CFO. Champion = CCO. 4–12 week cycle: DPA negotiation, IT SOC2 review, vendor checklist. EU data residency is non-negotiable. |
| **WHERE** | Money20/20, Finovate, VC portfolio Slacks. Reads Sifted and Fintech Nexus. Trusts founders one or two rounds ahead and specialist lawyers. Uses ChatGPT or Claude for regulatory research. |

## B2C example — longevity and preventive health app

| | |
|---|---|
| **WHO** | 35–42, male-skewing (70/30). Urban professional in tech or finance in London, Amsterdam or Berlin. €80K+ income. Data-driven, tracks HRV and glucose, wears Oura or Whoop. Sees health as an engineering problem. Wants to perform at 60 like they do at 35. |
| **WHAT** | Uses 4–6 disconnected apps (Oura, MyFitnessPal, Apple Health). Cobbles protocols together from podcasts and Reddit. Self-experiments with supplements. Spends 2–3 hours a week managing health data. Discovers through a Huberman or Attia mention or biohacking meetups. |
| **WHY** | No way to connect scattered data into one actionable view. Anxiety from tracking without understanding. Trigger: a bad blood test, turning 35 or 40, a friend diagnosed with something preventable, or a new wearable. |
| **HOW** | Researches for 2–4 weeks. Reads reviews, watches deep-dives, compares three options. Decides alone or with a partner. Sceptical of health claims: wants to see the study. Subscribes within two weeks if it clicks. |
| **WHERE** | Huberman Lab, The Peter Attia Drive, Rhonda Patrick. r/longevity, r/biohacking, Quantified Self meetups. Trusts Attia, Huberman, Sinclair and their functional medicine doctor. Not influencers. |

## What the examples do right

- Numbers everywhere: headcount, ARR, age range, hours per week, fine sizes, cycle length.
- Named places and people in WHERE.
- A trigger that is an event.
- The buyer and the champion separated (B2B).
- The status quo named, so the competitor is clear: manual work, or a stack of apps.
