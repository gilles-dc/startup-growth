# ICP — worked examples

From Gilles's *Customer, Problem, Value* workshop: every sub-question answered for one B2B and one B2C case, then the one-slide summary. Specific enough that a stranger could find this person.

The numbers in these examples are illustrations, not data. In your own output, tag every number that has no source (GUESS), in the tables and in the summary, as the skill's rules say.

# B2B — EU fintech compliance automation tool

## 1. WHO

| Sub-question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Vertical and stage | Series A–B EU fintech, 20–80 employees, €1M–10M ARR. Expanding into new regulated markets. |
| Company size | Backed by Seedcamp, Earlybird or equivalent EU VC. Not yet large enough for a dedicated compliance team. |
| Internal anatomy | Just hired first CCO. Engineering is 50–60% of headcount. No compliance tooling — runs on Sheets + Notion. |
| Target audience | Sells to regulated SMEs or consumers. Compliance burden scales with every new customer and market. |

## 2. WHAT

| Sub-question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Current solutions / existing stack | Google Sheets for tracking, email for evidence. Maybe a legacy GRC tool never properly configured. |
| Status quo | New category purchase — replacing one person's manual process, not a competitor. Never evaluated this software. |
| Discovery path | VC portfolio intro: "Two companies use them." Peer rec at Money20/20. LinkedIn only if it names their exact regulatory context. |

## 3. WHY

| Sub-question | Answer |
|---|---|
| The core problem | Preparing for regulatory audit in new EU market. Compliance consuming 20+ hrs/week of a senior hire. |
| Cost of inaction | Fines: €50K–€2M per violation. Loss of payment licence (existential). Failed Series B due to compliance flag in DD. |
| The motivation | Existential risk removal, not efficiency. Not trying to save €5K/month — trying to not lose their licence. |
| Budget and ownership | Buyer = COO/CFO (budget). Champion = new CCO (daily pain). €20K–€80K/yr for compliance tooling. |
| The buying trigger | Regulatory notice received. OR Series A closed with compliance condition. OR first compliance hire just made. |

## 4. HOW

| Sub-question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Decision logic | Buyer = COO/CFO. Champion = CCO. Legal reviews DPA. IT checks SOC2. Champion must sell internally. |
| Purchase process | 4–12 week cycle: discovery call → DPA negotiation → IT security review → formal vendor checklist → sign. |
| Friction points | EU data residency required. SOC2 non-negotiable. DPA negotiation alone takes 2–4 weeks. |

## 5. WHERE

| Sub-question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Content they consume | Sifted, Fintech Nexus, Nik Milanović newsletter. Fintech Insider podcast. LinkedIn: Simon Taylor, Lex Sokolin. |
| Communities | VC portfolio Slack groups. Fintech Founders communities. WhatsApp groups with peer founders from same vertical. |
| Events | Money20/20 Amsterdam, Finovate Europe, Slush Helsinki. Most important meetings happen in hallways. |
| Who they trust | Founders 1–2 rounds ahead. Lead VC partner. Specialist lawyers (Bird & Bird, Osborne Clarke). Former regulators. |

# B2C — longevity and preventive health app

## 1. WHO

| Sub-question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Demographics | 35–42, male-skewing (70/30). Urban: London, Amsterdam, Berlin. Works in tech or finance. €80K+ income. |
| Psychographics | Data-driven, optimises sleep & nutrition. Tracks HRV/glucose. Sees health as engineering problem, not vanity. |
| Behavioural signals | Wears Oura/Whoop/Apple Watch. Regular blood panels. Active on longevity Twitter/Reddit. Tries new protocols. |
| Aspirational self | Wants to perform at 60 like they do at 35. References Attia, Huberman, Sinclair. Sees peers ageing and refuses. |

## 2. WHAT

| Sub-question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Current solutions / existing stack | Uses 4–6 apps: Oura for sleep, MyFitnessPal for food, Apple Health aggregator. No single platform connects them. |
| Status quo | Cobbles together protocols from podcasts + Reddit. Self-experiments with supplements. Spends 2–3 hrs/week managing health data. |
| Discovery path | Podcast mention by Huberman/Attia. Reddit deep-dive threads. Peer recommendation at biohacking meetups. Never a Google ad. |

## 3. WHY

| Sub-question | Answer |
|---|---|
| The core problem | No way to connect scattered health data into one actionable view. Flying blind on long-term risk factors. |
| Cost of inaction | Missed early warning signs. Wasted money on wrong supplements. Anxiety from tracking without understanding. |
| The motivation | Wants to perform, not just survive. Not sick — refuses to become sick. Proactive, not reactive. |
| Budget and ownership | Already spends €200–€500/month on health stack. Price-insensitive if ROI is clear. Decides alone. |
| The buying trigger | Bad blood test result. Turning 35/40. Friend diagnosed with something preventable. New wearable purchase. |

## 4. HOW

| Sub-question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Decision logic | Researches for 2–4 weeks. Reads reviews, watches YouTube deep-dives. Compares 3 options. Decides alone or with partner. |
| Purchase process | Sees mention → checks site → reads 3–5 pieces of content → tries free/trial → subscribes within 2 weeks if it clicks. |
| Friction points | Sceptical of health claims ("show me the study"). Another subscription. Integration with existing wearables. |

## 5. WHERE

| Sub-question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Content they consume | Huberman Lab, Peter Attia Drive, Rhonda Patrick. Longevity subreddits, Bryan Johnson's Blueprint protocol. |
| Communities | r/longevity, r/biohacking. Quantified Self meetups. Premium Discord groups on health optimisation. |
| Events | Biohacking conferences (Health Optimisation Summit). Longevity Summit. CrossFit/functional fitness events. |
| Who they trust | Attia, Huberman, Sinclair. Their functional medicine doctor. Friends with similar optimisation mindset. Not influencers. |

## What the examples do right

- Every sub-question answered, in one or two dense sentences.
- Numbers everywhere: headcount, ARR, age range, hours per week, spend per month, fine sizes, cycle length.
- Named brands, tools, investors, people, events.
- The edge drawn: what they won't do or won't trust.
- A trigger that is an event.
- The buyer and the champion separated (B2B).
- The status quo named, so the competitor is clear: manual work, or a stack of apps.
