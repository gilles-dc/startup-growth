# Value propositions — worked examples

From Gilles De Clerck's *Customer, Problem, Value* and *Landing Pages* workshops.

## From bad alternative to value proposition (Landing Pages workshop)

A value proposition matches product quality to customer benefit.
- Product quality: "Here's what our product can do."
- Value prop: "Here's what **you** can do with our product."

**One quality, several benefits.** Imagine the product is fast:
| benefit | value prop |
|---|---|
| quicker output | "Get work done faster." |
| greater output | "Get more work done." |
| more efficiency | "Save yourself time." |

Imagine it's secure:
| benefit | value prop |
|---|---|
| protection | "If you lose your card, freeze it from the app." |
| control | "Turn off contactless, online payments and withdrawals from the app." |

### Generating value props

1. What bad alternatives do people resort to without your product?
2. How is your product better than that alternative?
3. Write it as an action statement: that's the value prop.

## The world without and with (Customer, Problem, Value workshop)

**Revolut**
| World without | World with |
|---|---|
| Can't open a bank account without a street address or credit history. | Open your account in seconds. No street address or credit checks. |
| Losing money on hidden exchange fees every time you travel. | Spend abroad at the real exchange rate with no hidden fees. |
| Waiting 3–5 days for international bank transfers. | Send money internationally in seconds. |
| Managing separate accounts at multiple banks for different currencies. | Hold and exchange 30+ currencies in one app. |

**Duolingo**: world without: "Expensive language courses and subscriptions." · "In-person courses with long-term commitments." · "Boring, textbook-style language learning."

**Be specific. Quantify.**
| Vague | Specific |
|---|---|
| "Save time on invoicing." | "Reduce invoicing from 3 hours/week to 15 minutes." |
| "Better exchange rates." | "€400/month saved on currency exchange." |
| "Better sleep." | "Increase deep sleep by 22% within 30 days." |
| "Cheaper than alternatives." | "10x lower cost than agency-built solutions." |

**Relieve a pain or create a gain**
- Relieve a pain: "Invoices reconciled automatically. 3 hours/week back to your team." · "One dashboard that connects your sleep, blood work, and supplements — so you know what to change."
- Create a gain: "Onboard new clients in 1 day instead of 2 weeks." · "Increase your deep sleep by 22% within 30 days — measured through your existing wearable."

## Statements

**Problem statement** = the what (quantified) + the why (underlying mechanics).
- Revolut: "Sending money abroad or spending in foreign currencies means hidden exchange fees, slow transfers, and juggling multiple bank accounts — because traditional banks profit from the opacity of cross-border transactions and have no incentive to make it cheaper or faster."
- SaaS onboarding: "B2B SaaS companies (ACV <€30k) lose about 8% of revenue — €1.6m on a €20m base — because 55% of new users quit before value. The current onboarding engine is broken and costly: onboarding tours are generic and break with product updates, and personalised human-led onboarding can't scale past a few big accounts."

**Value proposition statement** = the value (quantified outcome) + the mechanism.
- Revolut: "Revolut lets you spend, send, and exchange money globally at the real exchange rate with no hidden fees. It works by connecting directly to interbank rates and consolidating 30+ currencies in one app — replacing slow bank transfers and €400+/month in exchange markups with instant, transparent transactions."
- Duolingo: "Duolingo lets anyone learn a new language for free, at their own pace, from anywhere. It works through bite-sized, gamified lessons built around real-world conversations — replacing expensive courses and rigid schedules with 5 minutes a day on your phone."

| Product | The value | The mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Airbnb | Experience new cities like a local. No minimum stays. | An online rental marketplace with thousands of options in your area. |
| Revolut | Get paid back by your friends instantly. No fees. | A money transfer platform that instantly connects you and your friend's banks. |
| Webflow | Build your own website in 15 minutes. No code. | A website building platform that uses plug-and-play tech to help you launch quickly. |
| Robinhood | Stock trading without fees. No minimums. | An e-trading platform that allows for instant investments without trading fees. |
| Slack | Keep your business communication organised in one place. For free. | An all-in-one communication platform that organises conversations into channels and threads. |

## Headers with a hook (Landing Pages workshop)

Descriptive, while concise. Hook them, or lose them for good. Be specific; don't be vague ("Improve your workflow!"). If a visitor reads only this, would they know what you're selling? Make them want to scroll, but don't require them to scroll.

How to write killer headers:
1. Identify how users get value from the product (the vault).
2. Add a hook to keep them reading: **boldly address likely scepticism**, or make a bold, specific claim.
3. Speak directly to the customer persona.

**Hook: address the main objection.** "Build your own website." triggers:
- "But I don't know how to code."
- "This'll take too long. I'm not a designer."
- "This will be limited like other such tools."

| Objection | Updated header |
|---|---|
| Don't know how to code | Build your own website. Without code. |
| It'll take too long | Build your own website in 20 minutes. No code. |
| Low functionality | Build a custom website in 20 minutes. No code. |

| Value prop | Objection | Value + hook |
|---|---|---|
| Rank higher on Google | "I suck at SEO." | You don't have to be an SEO pro to rank higher on Google. |
| Ace the SAT | "I bet it's hard work." | Ace the SAT with just 10 minutes of studying per day. |
| ED pills | "I don't want to ask my doctor." | Erectile dysfunction pills. Delivered to your door. |

| Product | Original | Objection | Updated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Airbnb | Experience new cities like a local. | Only for long stays | Experience new cities like a local. No minimum stays. |
| Dropbox | Upload your files to the cloud automatically. | Security and privacy risks | … Chosen by Fortune 500s for superior security. |
| Deliveroo | Get your favourite meals in one click. | High delivery costs | … No extra fees. |
| Webflow | Launch your website yourself. | I can't code | Build your own website in 15 minutes. No code. |
| Robinhood | Trade stocks for free. | There's a minimum size | Stock trading without fees. No minimums. |
| Slack | Communicate effectively with everyone in one place. | It'll be expensive | Communicate with everyone in one place. Free for teams. |

Find objections by asking customers: "What almost stopped you from buying?" and "Why do you think others haven't bought from us yet?" Address the **major** objection in the header; the rest go in the features.

Several personas on one page: **"choose your own adventure"**: let visitors pick their persona at the top and route them to the matching section.

### Subheader

Once visitors understand **what** the product does, the subheader specifies **how**. When you promise amazing value, visitors think "OK, but how?":
- How does the product work, exactly?
- Which feature makes the claim believable?

Structure: header = 1. value prop · subheader = 2. introduce the product + 3. how it creates the value prop. One or two sentences.

|---|---|
| How small brands sell more online | Privy's conversion, email marketing and text messaging tools help you get more customers from your traffic. |
| Your most restful sleep is just a sip away | Calming teas that help you relax, unwind and drift into a deep, restorative sleep. |
| Ace the SAT with just 10 minutes of studying a day | 10-minute microlessons designed to boost confidence and make SAT strategies easy to remember. |

| Keep your business communication organised in one place. For free. | Slack is an all-in-one communication platform that organises your conversations into channels, threads and direct messages. |
